POPIA Compliance for Ignite Fintech Payments
Purpose of POPIA
The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) is designed to protect the privacy and personal information of individuals. It requires organizations to collect, store, process, and share personal information in a lawful, secure, and responsible manner. 【1-d0ef89】【2-50376d】

How POPIA Applies to Ignite Fintech Payments
Ignite Fintech Payments operates as a digital payments company that processes electronic financial transactions and stores customer information. Every payment made through the platform generates personal and financial data that must be protected from unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

Because the company collects, stores, and processes large amounts of customer information, POPIA is one of the most important compliance requirements for the organization.
